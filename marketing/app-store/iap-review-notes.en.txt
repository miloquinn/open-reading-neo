This is a one-time, non-consumable lifetime Premium purchase (com.niki.xxread.premium.lifetime), with no subscription or automatic renewal.

On macOS, open Settings, enter the account center, sign in, and open Premium. The purchase screen explains the benefits and displays the localized App Store price. Premium unlocks additional compatible book-source protocols and trusted local/private-network sources. After purchase, these options are available under Settings > Advanced Features and must be enabled by the user. The app does not supply commercial books or a bundled third-party source catalog.

Restore Purchases is available in the membership screen. Purchases are verified on the server and linked to the signed-in Open Reading account.
