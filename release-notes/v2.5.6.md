# v2.5.6 Release Notes

## Bug Fixes

### Restored the exact EPUB and TXT reading position

**Issue**: Leaving an EPUB or TXT reader and reopening it could restore only to the first page of the last-read chapter, rather than the page that was visible when the reader was closed. This was especially noticeable when the "Resume last reading" startup option was enabled.

**Solution**:

- Capture the currently visible page, including an in-progress horizontal page turn, before the reader route is dismissed.
- Persist a resume snapshot containing the book ID, chapter index, and canonical in-chapter text locator.
- Apply the resume snapshot during startup so it takes precedence over a potentially older database progress record.
- Serialize resume writes so page turns, lifecycle pauses, and reading-state updates cannot overwrite a newer position.

**Impact**:

- EPUB and TXT books reopen at the exact page and text position where reading stopped.
- The "Resume last reading" option now resumes within a chapter instead of falling back to its first page.
- Horizontal page turns at chapter boundaries remain stable while the reader state is saved.

## Technical Details

- Added a durable reading-session snapshot with `bookId`, `chapterIndex`, and `canonicalLocator`.
- Reader-exit progress now resolves the active or pending horizontal page before persistence.
- Startup resume reconciles the session snapshot with stored reading progress.
- Resume persistence is sequenced to preserve the newest reader position.
